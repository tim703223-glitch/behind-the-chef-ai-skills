# Social caption -- local-business-seo-audit

I was 40 minutes from walking into a local restaurant with a one-page review for the owner, and I
asked Claude one question: "did you actually look at their social media before writing this?" It
hadn't. Every number came from a write-up six weeks old, presented as current.

So we did it live, and that's when the real lesson showed up. First attempt at auditing their website
reported every page title empty and no SEO anywhere. Looked like a damning finding. It was completely
wrong -- modern site builders serve an empty shell to a script, so "nothing set up" and "you used the
wrong method" look identical. The titles were all there the whole time.

Three more like that followed: a page that looked blank was just slow to load, a shortcut pulled the
wrong account's bio entirely, and fixing three too-long descriptions quietly created a fourth.

Every single failure looked like a result. That's the whole reason this skill exists, and it's now
eight rules about not trusting your own first answer.

Full build write-up, including the two pages of theirs that really are blank and the wrong ZIP code
we found: https://github.com/tim703223-glitch/behind-the-chef-ai-skills/blob/main/skills/local-business-seo-audit/WRITEUP.md
